# FEATURES 
- You are allowed to suggest improvements or changes to any features or architechture of this project. Critically evaluate the usefulness of each tool, and give feedback where it is due, with helpful suggestions. Each screen should have as minimal scrolling (on desktop) as possible, creating a more dashboard/app feel rather than a website. Use more tabs or a carousell system (suggestion) to keep information contained on the screen without scrolling.

- The product: A Madden 27 "Franchise Manager" which gives me an overview of the franchise. It should act as a planning tool and not be reliant on the game's save files. 
- It acts as a way to plan the game without actually playing the game. This allows me to be efficient when i actually get on the game, to re-order the depth chart or formation subs etc.
- For the purposes of this website, the default team should be my favourite team: Atlanta Falcons. However, it should be easily translatable to other teams if I start a new franchise with a different team. The database should work per franchise (is this feasible?), as each new save will branch off into its own reality.

- The main features of the app include: "Depth Chart": being able to plan my depth chart (as it is set in Madden 27, with primary positions (WR, REDG, WILL etc) and secondary positions (SLWR, SUBLB, NT etc.)). The depth chart should be set in a "list" view per position. Research on how Madden 26/27 and CFB 26/27 depth charts work in order to accurately replicate this feature. Remember "practice squad" as part of the depth chart/my team
- Another main feature "Formation Subs": I should easily be able to see how my team lines up in various formation diagrams. These formation diagrams are updated based on the data in the depth chart. Get the labels right from [Civil.gg], they are essential in translating the formation and the position labels from a static image to a dynamic, updateable diagram in the website. My players' name, number and ovr should appear on the labelled spots. If i want to edit the personnel in a specific formation, i should be able to do so. Research on Madden 26/27 and CFB 26/27 Formation Subs to get a better idea of what i am trying to articulate.
- Useable on Multiple Devices: The web app must be mobile-friendly to make adjustments and view the call-sheet on the fly without access to the game.
- Usable without owning the game: All player data comes from a real published ratings dataset (see Data); the app must work fully with no console/save file attached.
- "Checklist": A list of changes i made in the website that i have yet to implement into the game. gives me real, actionable items to efficiently update my depth chart/formation subs/trades in the game.

- More complex features: "GM War Room": gives me the opportunity to see my current roster, number of contract years left, cap hit, who is due for an extension, etc. Also gives me the ability to scout players on other teams based on scheme fit and certain attributes, or by searching name. I should be able to log trades, and my team should be updated after making a trade.
- More complex features: "Coach View": 1 tabs for now: "Scheme and Playbook" "Scheme and Playbook": should give me a broad overview of the general scheme fit of my team, which players are the correct archetype, etc. suggets more tabs for "Coach view".
- More complex features: "Gameday": What i will use on game day. It should contain a drive playcaller. This drive playcaller should give me ideas on what to call in each drive, both on offense and defense. On offense, it should have a certain theme, eg: "Establish under center (singleback) identity" or "Establish run game" or "Show jet motion" (just ideas for naming). Under each drive theme/identity, i should have suggested plays put into buckets of down and distance. This way, i can learn to call the drive mostly from the formation screen instead of following coach suggestions. Showing similar looks pre-snap to the opponents is a big part of what i am trying to accomplish, while being able to disguise my plays. This drive playcaller engine should do comprehensive research before attempting to start creating the suggestion logic. Allow me to favourite certain formations as my "bread and butter" offense/defense. On defense, have a couple of focuses like "stopping the run" etc when planning out playcalls during a drive. It could be based on down and distance, but also based on the offense's personnel (i should not match empty backfield offense with a 3-4 defense, creates mismatches for the offense where a WR is against a LB).

## Data
- The site should import all players in the Madden 27 database (~3116 players), and all their stats/attributes, including height and weight, and jersey number. These should be editable from the site as the franchise save progresses (in the event i trade for a player and he changes jersey number, or changes occur during the course of the franchise).
- The site should import all playbooks from [Civil.gg](https://www.civil.gg/playbooks/madden). Most importantly, it should translate the formation diagrams into interactive/dynamic diagrams on the site that update automatically based on the depth chart. Note that in most formations, "WR3" is also listed as "SLWR1" in the depth chart. If i update the depth chart and change my WR1, for example, the new WR1 should show on the formation diagrams.
- Scale notes: the real dataset is ~3,116 players across 32 clubs (~1,196 of them unsigned free agents), 53 visible attributes per player, 36 player archetypes, 21 team schemes, 6 seeded playbooks with 34 formations, and a test suite of ~380 tests over the domain logic.
